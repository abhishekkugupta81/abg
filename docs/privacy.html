Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Overview

Emojitsu is a game where players can battle using emojis, play against another player or a bot, and compete on the leaderboard. This Privacy Policy explains the types of information that may be used to operate the game and provide support.

Information We Collect

The app may use gameplay-related data such as scores, trophies, match results, leaderboard rank, selected emoji, and game progress. This information helps the game run core features and show player progress.

Leaderboard and Gameplay Data

Leaderboard and gameplay data may be used to display rankings, track results, unlock progress, and support player-versus-player or bot gameplay. Some information may be associated with a player profile, account identifier, or device-related identifier when needed for game features.

Game Center / Apple Services

If Emojitsu uses Apple services such as Game Center or the App Store, Apple may process information according to Apple's own privacy policies and service terms. Please review Apple's policies for details about how Apple handles that information.

How We Use Information

We use gameplay-related information to operate the game, show leaderboard results, improve gameplay, troubleshoot issues, respond to support requests, and maintain a fair game experience.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Gameplay information may be shared with service providers or platform services only as needed to operate the app, provide leaderboard features, process support requests, or comply with applicable requirements.

Data Retention

Gameplay and support-related information may be kept for as long as needed to provide the game, maintain leaderboard history, resolve issues, improve the app, or meet reasonable operational needs.

Children's Privacy

Emojitsu is intended to be used in a way that is appropriate for its App Store age rating. If you believe a child has provided information that should be reviewed or deleted, please contact us using the email below.

User Requests

You can contact us to ask privacy questions or request deletion of information associated with your gameplay or support request. We may need enough details to locate the relevant information.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact us at support.emojitsu@gmail.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised update date.
