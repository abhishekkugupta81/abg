Emojitsu Support

Thanks for playing Emojitsu. If something is not working as expected, or if you have ideas that would make the game better, we want to hear from you.

Contact Support

You can contact support for bugs, feedback, feature requests, leaderboard issues, or gameplay problems.

support.emojitsu@gmail.com

When reporting an issue, please include:

  • Device model
  • iOS version
  • App version
  • Description of the issue
  • Screenshot or screen recording if available

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play Emojitsu?

Pick an emoji, start a clash, and try to win the match through quick gameplay choices.

Can I play against a bot?

Yes. Bot Clash lets you play against a bot opponent when you want a fast solo match.

How does the leaderboard work?

The leaderboard may use gameplay results such as scores, match outcomes, trophies, and rank to show player progress.

How do I report a problem?

Email support.emojitsu@gmail.com with your device details, app version, and a clear description of the issue.
